# Miftāḥ: store listing

**App name (30 max):** Miftāḥ: Islamic Homeschool
**Short description (80 max):** An Islamic school from age 2 to 18. Free, private, works offline.
**Category:** Education · **Content rating:** Everyone · **Tags:** Designed for Families

## Full description

Every skill is a key. Every lesson is a door.

Miftāḥ is a complete Islamic school day for children aged 2 to 11. Open it and your child sees one thing: today's path. Remember, recite, learn something new, then go and do it for real.

**A full school day, fully taught**
- Qurʾān: all of Juzʾ ʿAmma and al-Fātiḥah, with real recitation by al-Ḥuṣarī and al-ʿAfāsī, and every word tappable
- Arabic: from the first letter to real Qurʾānic words, grammar and root families
- Living Islam: faith, wuḍūʾ and ṣalāh, adab, daily duʿāʾ, hadith, and the life of the Prophet ﷺ
- Maths: counting to fractions and percent, built with your hands
- English: phonics, decodable reading, grammar, comprehension
- Science: predict first, then test

**Teaching that respects how children learn**
- One rhythm in every lesson: remember, meet, name it, try together, on your own, make it, keep it
- Real things before symbols, and missions that send children off the screen
- Keys dim as memory fades, and come back just in time
- Mistakes are met with a calmer look, not a red cross. There are no points and no scores
- Three stages, and a separate level in every room. A child can be ahead in maths and just starting Arabic
- For the very youngest: a card for the grown-up, one big picture for the child, then back to real play

**The Qurʾān is handled with care**
No games, no scrambled verses, no effects. Memorising runs on sabaq, sabqī and manzil. A person listens, or your child honestly checks. No machine ever marks recitation.

**For parents**
See what is proven, not just what was tapped. What to talk about this week, time and attendance, observations, recitations to hear, and a printable record. Record your own voice over any letter or sound.

**Sound and pictures**
No music and no chimes. Nothing drawn has a face. Prophets, angels and Companions are never depicted.

Free. No ads. No account. Your records stay on your phone, and lessons work offline. The internet is used only for a YouTube video a parent adds, and to download Qur’an recordings once, from everyayah.com and quran.com.

## Data safety answers
No data collected by Miftāḥ. No data shared. The internet permission is used only to play YouTube videos a parent has added, and to download Qur’an recordings once; YouTube has its own privacy policy, and the private youtube-nocookie player is used. The microphone is used only when a parent records a voice or a child records a recitation, and files stay on the device.
