Miftāḥ privacy

Miftāḥ has no account, ads or analytics. The app uses the internet to download recitations, load thumbnails and videos for YouTube links a parent adds, and check GitHub Releases for updates. The weekly update check is optional and off by default. An imported simulation may load scripts or fonts from supported providers. Those providers receive requests from your device under their own privacy policies.

The app stores learning progress, parent notes and recordings on your device. If Android cloud backup is enabled, it can back up learning records and settings. Miftāḥ excludes voice recordings, added media and pairing keys from Android cloud backup. Android can transfer recordings and media directly to a new device.

The app uses the microphone when someone starts a voice or recitation recording. Families can exchange learning records and recordings between paired devices on their home network or share a family file. A parent can also export a backup and choose where to keep it.
