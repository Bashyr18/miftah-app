Miftāḥ privacy

Miftāḥ does not collect, send or share anything. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. It has permission to use the internet for two purposes only: to download Qur’an recordings once from everyayah.com and quran.com, which see only your device’s request for that file, and to play a YouTube video that a parent has chosen to add. All other network access is blocked inside the app. When such a video plays, YouTube receives what it normally receives from any embedded player, under its own privacy policy. If no video is added, nothing ever leaves the device.

A child's progress, a parent's notes and any voice recordings are stored only on the phone. They are removed when the app is uninstalled, or when a parent removes a child.

The microphone is used only when a parent chooses to record their voice, or when a recitation recording is started. Those recordings stay on the phone.

A parent can make a backup file and decide where to keep it.
