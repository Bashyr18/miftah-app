# Miftāḥ: the design for each age

One app, five ways of being used. The stage comes from the child's age (`A.stage`, `core.js`), sets `body[data-age]`, and each row here maps to code.

## By interface

| Interface | 2 to 4, Little Steps | 5 to 7, Explorers | 8 to 11, Seekers | 12 to 14, Builders | 15 to 18, Scholars |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Who runs the app | A grown-up beside them | Child, grown-up nearby | Child follows a plan | Student adjusts the plan | Student runs their own study |
| Home | One big card, no reading needed | Courtyard, one next door, spoken | Courtyard, next door, day path | Clean list of today, goal, exams | Same, with exam countdown first |
| Subjects | None; the app chooses | Portal rooms, year map | Portal rooms, year map | Subject list with progress, syllabus by year | Subject list with progress, syllabus by year |
| Progress | Stickers of real play | Key ring | Key ring and lanterns | Record with % remembered, transcript | Record with % remembered, transcript |
| Opening a lesson | Plain | 3D door once a day, then plain | 3D door once a day, then plain | Plain, door can be switched on | Plain, door can be switched on |
| Finishing a lesson | Warm praise for effort | The forged key, still | The forged key, still | Plain result, and where it is used next | Plain result, and where it is used next |
| Voice | Everything spoken | Prompts spoken, parent can turn off | Spoken only when tapped | Spoken only when tapped | Spoken only when tapped |
| Touch sounds | Off, unless a parent turns them on | Off | Off | Off | Off |
| Lab of simulations | None | From Year 2 | Yes | Yes, plus their own | Yes, plus their own |
| Testing | None | Unit checks | Unit checks, year exams, certificates | Year exams, timed papers | Year exams, timed papers, past-paper practice |
| Arabic new starter | Sounds | Letters by tracing | Older beginners’ road with a pen | Older beginners’ road with a pen | Older beginners’ road with a pen |
| Touch size | 72 px | 60 px | 50 px | 46 px | 44 px |
| Type and shape | Largest, round | Large, round | Medium, round | Smaller, squarer, no gradients | Smallest, squarer, focused |
| Movement breaks | Led by a grown-up | Every 11 minutes | Every 18 minutes | Self-managed | Self-managed |
| Length of day | 10 min | 20 min | 40 min | 75 min | 120 min, or their own timetable |
| Parent sees | All of it, they are there | Everything | Everything | Progress and worries | Summary; work only if shared |
| Sensitive topics | None | None | None | Taught, with a parent supervising | Taught, with a parent supervising |

## By kind of topic

| Kind of topic | How it is taught |
|---|---|
| New maths idea | Objects or a picture first, then symbols, then fresh questions until it holds |
| A skill to automate (facts, tables) | Short, frequent, timed from about age 8 |
| Hard problems | Weekly from about age 12, with being stuck treated as the training |
| Science | Predict, test, explain, with a real experiment wherever safe |
| Building | Design, build, test to failure, improve |
| Religious text | Real recordings and verified text only; never gamified or machine-scored |
| Fiqh | Four schools with their evidence, then the majority or strongest evidence, by the way of the Salaf |
| Sensitive topics | With a parent, from an Islamic perspective |
| Written work | Written, then checked against what a good answer contains, then marked by a parent if shared |
