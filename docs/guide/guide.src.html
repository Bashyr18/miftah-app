Why it exists

Knowledge, kept and passed on

ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ ۝ عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ

Who taught by the pen, taught humanity what they knew not. Al-ʿAlaq 96:4–5 · The Clear Quran

No adverts, no account, no tracking. A family’s record lives on its own devices and moves between them only on the family’s own Wi-Fi, or in a file they send themselves. The code is open, so anyone can check it, learn from it and make it better.

Verified sourcesThe Qurʾān byte for byte from Tanzil, its translation from The Clear Quran, duʿāʾ from Ḥiṣn al-Muslim. Never clipped, never a game.
Honest learningA lesson counts only when the child shows it alone. Paper and on-screen work count only once a grown-up marks it.
PrivateLearning data stays on your devices. Phone and computer talk only at home, sealed with a key they alone share.
Open sourceMIT licensed. Build the Android and Windows apps yourself, from one shared core.
