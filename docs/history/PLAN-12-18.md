# Miftāḥ, ages 12 to 18: the plan

Status: superseded, 2026-11-06. This has been built. See `../AGE-UX.md` for the design for each age, `../ENGINE.md` for how it works, and `../CURRICULUM.md` for the lessons.

## The short version

Miftāḥ can last until university, but not by doing more of the same. Three things change after age 11:

1. **The learner changes.** A teenager should gradually own their plan. The parent moves from running the day to being kept informed.
2. **The app's job changes.** Up to about 14 the app can teach. From about 15, exam courses need textbooks, real labs, marked essays and sometimes tutors. There the app is best as the **study system around them**: planning, memory, practice, tracking and records. It should not claim to replace a qualification course.
3. **The engineering changes.** A record that must survive up to 16 years needs real storage, migration, sync and backup, and typing long answers needs a keyboard.

Recommended order: engine first, then Years 8 to 10 as full lessons, then Years 11 to 14 as guided study.

## Two new stages

Year numbers continue the existing rule, age minus 4. Age 12 is Year 8 and age 16 is Year 12.

| | Seekers (today) | **Stage 3: Builders** (working name) | **Stage 4: Scholars** (working name) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ages, years | 8 to 11, Years 4 to 7 | 12 to 14, Years 8 to 10 | 15 to 18, Years 11 to 14 |
| App's role | Teaches | Teaches, and starts handing over | Study system around books, teachers and exams |
| Day in the app | About 40 min | About 60 to 90 min | Set by the student's own timetable |
| Plan set by | Engine and parent | Engine proposes, student adjusts | Student, with the engine as advisor |
| Parent sees | Everything | Progress and worries, not every answer | Agreed summary and exam dates |
| Look and feel | Doors and courtyard | Quieter, door ritual optional | Focused study tool, door off by default |

**What stays:** the key as memory strength, which suits teenagers well. Spacing, retrieval practice and honest evidence are even more valuable here. The Qur'an is still never gamified or machine-scored.

**What grows up:** a key ring becomes a personal record of knowledge. Big milestones become certificates and a printable transcript. Movement breaks become self-managed.

## Curriculum map

| Room | Years 8 to 10: full lessons | Year 11: first exams | Years 12 to 14: chosen subjects |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Qur'an** | Hifz continues at the student's pace, tajwīd rules studied, meaning of what they memorise | Long-term review planner for many juzʾ | Whole-Qur'an revision cycle, a teacher's ijāzah recorded if they earn one |
| **Arabic** | Full grammar and morphology at beginner level, wide reading, writing | Understanding classical text with a dictionary; a language exam if chosen | Classical texts, balāghah, reading tafsīr and hadith in Arabic |
| **Islamic studies** | ʿAqīdah, fiqh of worship in the family's school, Seerah in depth, Islamic history | Ethics, contemporary questions, answering doubts | Introductions to uṣūl, ʿulūm al-Qur'ān, muṣṭalaḥ, marriage and family, money, a scholar-guided path |
| **Maths** | Algebra, geometry, trigonometry, probability, statistics | Exam course practice | Calculus, statistics, mechanics |
| **English** | Literature, rhetoric, essays, research | Exam course practice | Literature or language, academic writing |
| **Sciences** | Splits into biology, chemistry and physics, with safe home practicals | Exam course practice; labs need a real provider | Chosen sciences |
| **Our World** | History, geography, civics, economics | Chosen humanities | History, economics, politics, geography |
| **Computing** (new) | Logic, algorithms, programming | Optional exam course | Programming projects |
| **Life and future** (new) | First aid, cooking, budgeting, online safety, study skills | Choosing subjects and careers | University and scholarship applications, personal statement, interviews, zakāh on real money, contracts, living away from home with one's deen intact |

Exam specifications must be mapped from the current official documents when each year is built. Exam boards' wording and past papers are copyrighted, so the app would map to topics and link to official sources without copying them.

## Built to last 16 years

- **Storage:** move to a real database, with versioned migrations and tests that open old records.
- **Sync and backup:** automatic encrypted backup and several devices per student. A lost tablet must not lose ten years of work. This needs a decision about where data lives.
- **Content updates:** lessons arrive as packs without a reinstall, which also fits the size plan in `ENGINE.md`.
- **Keystore:** if the signing key is lost, the app can never be updated. It needs a private backup now.
- **Student accounts:** a teen's own passcode, and a clear agreement about what parents can see.
- **Platforms:** Android WebView will change a lot over 16 years, so the code must stay simple and free of fragile dependencies.

## Sensitive content

These need a scholar and a parent's choice, not my judgment:

- Puberty, purity rulings and modesty, which differ for boys and girls and should open only with a parent's approval.
- Marriage and family.
- Evolution, and modern conflicts such as Palestine.
- Differences between schools and groups.
- Mental health and safeguarding. The app should point students to trusted adults and real help, and must never act as a counsellor.

## Phases

1. **Foundations:** database, migrations, stages 3 and 4, pace profiles, years to 14, content packs, backup.
2. **New tools:** maths input, written answers, flashcards, teen planner.
3. **Years 8 and 9**, with the new look and handover.
4. **Year 10 and the whole-Qur'an hifz planner.**
5. **Desktop app**, moved up from "later" because essays need a keyboard.
6. **Year 11 exam support**, mapped to the chosen exam route.
7. **Years 12 to 14** subject choice, Life and future, university applications.
8. **Final size decision**, using the options in `ENGINE.md`.
